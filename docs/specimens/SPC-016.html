CON-020 · branch of SPC-016

A Topper with 4 Slots to Hold or Combine Chain

Challenge
Create an easily deployable way for communities to block/discourage traffic when needed by placing high visibility chain between two posts. Also useful as a guard chain along a series of posts to discourage people from wandering into the street. Removable cap prevents chain from easily being removed but does allow relatively quick access. Commercial versions of this design already exist. We just mean to produce a DIY version that's easy to replicate and made for Barcelona's specific common bollard dimensions.
Blocks
Tricky to construct using laser cut techniques, except maybe using a square design and UV/heat resistant plastics.
Materials
Worth trying a 3D printed version and plastic chain. Resilient version would need to be milled from HDPE or PVC, or cast aluminum. Maybe cast from mycelium in 3D printed molds for lightweight use.
